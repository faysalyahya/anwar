Research

Dissertation —
the Nador corridor

Oxford Brookes · 2025–2026

Supply chain analysis of AEOLON–NORDEX at the Port of Nador West Med

Inbound sourcing of raw materials from Asia and Europe, and outbound export of wind turbine blades to European markets.

The dissertation maps the supply chain of the AEOLON wind farm and the NORDEX N175 blade flow through the Port of Nador, applying Transaction Cost Economics (TCE) and the Resource-Based View (RBV) to the wind industry, project cargo logistics and emerging infrastructure.

It builds on first-hand fieldwork during the Somanador internship — the discharge of NORDEX N175 blades aboard the M/V Superfast Levante in November and December 2025 — and connects operational reality with the strategic implications of Nador West Med, the deep-water port opening 2026 that will reshape container and energy logistics across the Western Mediterranean.
